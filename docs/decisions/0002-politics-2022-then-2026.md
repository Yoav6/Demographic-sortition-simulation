# 0002 — Politics: the 2022 election now, the 2026 election when certified

* Status: accepted (2026-10)

## Context

The model needs a political attribute grounded in ballot data. The latest certified election
is the 25th Knesset (1 November 2022; [`src:cec_knesset25`](../../SOURCES.md#src-cec_knesset25)). The 26th Knesset election is on 27 October 2026
([JNS](https://www.jns.org/news/israel-news/knesset-votes-to-dissolve-formally-sets-oct-27-election-date)); certified
results are expected in mid-November 2026. Survey data on vote intention for 2026 (INES 2025, [`src:ines_2025`](../../SOURCES.md#src-ines_2025)) are
available but refer to party lists that will change.

## Decision

Model the **2022 vote** (with abstention, and "too young" for people under 18 on election day)
now. When the 2026 results are certified, add a `vote_2026` node estimated the same way (INES
2025 or 2026 for individual effects, calibrated to the official results by locality profile and
Arab area) and publish a new model version and a new edition of the default assembly.

## Consequences

* Because random numbers are per node, adding or replacing the vote node does not change anyone's
  other attributes in the published assembly (only nodes downstream of the vote — the media diet
  — can change).
* Until then the site describes votes as "in the 2022 election".
