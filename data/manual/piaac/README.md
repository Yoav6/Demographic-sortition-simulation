# PIAAC 2023 — population-group means (manual)

`boi_group_means.csv`: mean PIAAC 2022–23 scores of non-Haredi Jews, Arabs and Haredim in
Israel, as printed (labelled bars) in Figure 2b, page 6, of the Bank of Israel Research
Department's policy note 2024.03 ([`src:boi_piaac_note_2024`](../../../SOURCES.md#src-boi_piaac_note_2024)). The authors computed
them in the CBS research room; they are not published elsewhere. Ages 16–65.
